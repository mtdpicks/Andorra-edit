Andorra airport

Andorra has no airport of its own. Here are the nearest ones, the coaches that serve them, and a planner that tells you when to leave for your flight.

How early to get to the airport

  • Schengen flights (Spain, France and most of the EU): about 2 hours before departure.
  • UK, Ireland and other non-Schengen flights: about 2 hours 30 minutes, to allow for passport control.
  • Long haul: about 3 hours.
  • Busy days: add 30 minutes at weekends, on holidays and on the last day of ski weeks, when the border and roads are busy, and more if snow is forecast.

These are general guides; your airline's advice comes first.

Getting to the coach from your parish

Andorra Direct Bus leaves from the national bus station in Andorra la Vella, with a stop in Sant Julià de Lòria 10 minutes later. Andbus picks up at stops in villages across Andorra, from Pas de la Casa and Soldeu to Arinsal and Sant Julià. To reach the bus station from elsewhere, take a national bus line or a taxi. Andorra–La Seu d’Urgell airport

The nearest airport to Andorra is just over the border in Spain, about 15 km from the frontier and 30 to 40 minutes from Andorra la Vella by road. It’s small, with a handful of scheduled flights a week, and it handled 19,160 passengers in 2025, according to Alto.

Until the end of 2026, it has flights to Madrid and Palma de Mallorca under the current Air Nostrum contract. From 1 January 2027, the French airline Chalair takes over, with year round flights to Paris-Orly, Madrid and Palma and seasonal routes to Nantes, Lyon and Nice, TourMaG and Ulysse reported. Launch fares started from €55 one way to Palma, €67 to Madrid and €79 to Paris, and the government’s tender asks for at least two flights a week on each main route.

There’s no airport bus: book a taxi, from €31 to La Seu, or a transfer. See taxis in Andorra, and the airport’s own site for the current timetable.

Andorra airport questions

Does Andorra have an airport?

No. Andorra has no airport of its own. The nearest are Andorra–La Seu d'Urgell in Spain, about 30 to 40 minutes away, with a few flights a week, and the main gateways, Barcelona El Prat and Toulouse Blagnac, each about three hours away by direct coach.

How long is the coach from Andorra to Barcelona airport?

Andorra Direct Bus estimates about 3 hours 30 minutes from Andorra la Vella to Barcelona airport. Andbus also runs direct coaches to Terminals 1 and 2.

How early should I leave Andorra for my flight?

Work back from your flight: about 3 hours 30 minutes by coach to Barcelona airport, plus 2 to 3 hours at the airport depending on your destination, plus extra time at weekends or in snow. The planner on this page does the sums for you.

What is the nearest airport to Andorra?

Andorra–La Seu d'Urgell, in Spain about 15 km from the border, with flights to Madrid and Palma, and from January 2027 to Paris too. For most international flights, the nearest big airports are Toulouse, about 185 km away, and Barcelona, about 200 km.

Which airport is best for skiing in Andorra?

Toulouse is closest to Pas de la Casa, Grau Roig and Soldeu: Pas de la Casa is about two hours away by road. Barcelona has the most flights, and direct coaches to Andorra la Vella in about three to three and a half hours.

Can you fly from Andorra to Paris?

From 1 January 2027, the French airline Chalair is due to fly twice a week between Andorra–La Seu d'Urgell and Paris-Orly, alongside year round flights to Madrid and Palma, according to the travel trade press.

Where is Andorra?

Everything new visitors ask: where it is, who lives there, what they speak, and the facts that make it unlike anywhere else in Europe.

Between France and Spain

Andorra sits high in the eastern Pyrenees. France lies to the north and east, and Spain's Catalonia to the south and west. The country is landlocked, almost entirely mountainous, and about three hours by road from both Barcelona and Toulouse.

It has seven parishes (parròquies): Canillo, Encamp, Ordino, La Massana, Andorra la Vella, Sant Julià de Lòria and Escaldes-Engordany. Its head of state is shared by two co-princes, the President of France and the Bishop of Urgell, an arrangement with roots in the 13th century.

Map of Andorra: the seven parishes

Andorra is made up of seven parishes, or parròquies, each run by its own council, the comú. Most people live along the valley floors, where the Valira d’Orient and the Valira del Nord meet at Escaldes-Engordany and flow south into Spain as the Gran Valira.

Andorra la Vella and Escaldes-Engordany have grown into one town in the central valley. The CG-1 follows the river south to the Spanish border at La Farga de Moles, the CG-2 climbs east through Encamp and Canillo to Pas de la Casa and France, and side roads run north to La Massana, Ordino and the high valleys.

Who lives in Andorra

On 31 July 2026 Andorra passed 90,000 official residents for the first time: 90,021 people, up 1.9% in a year. Foreign nationals make up about 55% of the population. Andorran nationals number 40,128, followed by Spanish (21,225), Portuguese (about 8,300) and French (4,263) residents.

The flag of Andorra

The flag has three vertical bands of blue, yellow and red, with the middle band slightly wider. The coat of arms at its centre (simplified here) combines the mitre and crozier of the Bishop of Urgell, the red and gold bars of Foix and Catalonia, and the two cows of Béarn. Beneath it is the motto Virtus Unita Fortior, "strength united is stronger".

Things you may not know

Two heads of state, neither residentThe co-princes are the President of the French Republic and the Bishop of Urgell, whose seat is in La Seu d'Urgell in Spain.

No airport, no railwayThe nearest railway station is L'Hospitalet-près-l'Andorre on the French side. The nearest airport, Andorra–La Seu d'Urgell, is in Spain.

A UNESCO valleyThe Madriu-Perafita-Claror valley, a glacial landscape of summer pastures and stone shepherds' huts, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Hot water under the capitalSprings in Escaldes-Engordany emerge at around 68–71°C. You can see the water for free at the Roc del Metge fountain, and it also supplies Caldea.

A parliament in a houseCasa de la Vall, a stone house from the 16th century in Andorra la Vella, was the seat of the General Council for centuries and can be toured today.

A 603 m bridgeThe Tibetan bridge in Canillo, opened in 2022, runs 603 m across the Vall del Riu at up to 158 m above the ground. It is one of the longest pedestrian suspension bridges in the world.

Wine in the mountainsFive cellars make Andorran wine: Borda Sabaté 1944, Casa Auvinyà, Casa Beal, Celler Mas Berenguer and Casus Belli.

Renewable ski liftsOrdino Arcalís generates all of its own energy, including from a small hydroelectric plant, and Pal Arinsal has its own solar park.

Questions people ask

Is Andorra a country?

Yes. Andorra is a sovereign principality and a member of the United Nations. It is a parliamentary co-principality whose two co-princes are the President of France and the Bishop of Urgell.

What is the capital of Andorra?

Andorra la Vella. At 1,023 m above sea level it is the highest capital city in Europe.

Is Andorra in the EU?

No. Andorra is not a member of the European Union, but it uses the euro under a monetary agreement with the EU.

What language is spoken in Andorra?

Catalan is the only official language. Spanish and French are widely spoken, and English is common in hotels, ski schools and the main shops.

How big is Andorra?

Andorra covers 468 square kilometres. Almost all of it is mountainous, with its highest point at Comapedrosa (2,942 m).

What currency does Andorra use?

The euro. Andorra uses it under a monetary agreement with the EU, although it is not an EU member.

Is Andorra part of Spain or France?

Neither. Andorra is an independent country between them, with its own parliament and government. Its two co-princes are the President of France and the Bishop of Urgell, whose seat is in Spain.

Where is Andorra on the map?

In the eastern Pyrenees, with France to the north and east and Spain to the south and west. Andorra la Vella is about 200 km by road from Barcelona and 185 km from Toulouse.

How many parishes does Andorra have?

Seven: Canillo, Encamp, Ordino, La Massana, Andorra la Vella, Sant Julià de Lòria and Escaldes-Engordany. Each has its own council, the comú.

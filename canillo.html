Canillo

The high valley between Encamp and Soldeu: the Tibetan bridge and Roc del Quer, the sanctuary of Meritxell, the Incles valley, and two of Andorra’s best loved ski villages.

Canillo at a glance

Canillo is a village at about 1,500 m on the main road from Andorra la Vella to France, and its parish runs on up the valley to El Tarter and Soldeu.

Side valleys open off it at Incles, Ransol and Montaup, and Meritxell, Prats and L’Aldosa sit on the slopes above the road. With 6,593 residents it’s one of the smaller parishes, but it grew faster than any other over the past year, by 5%. Come in summer for the walks, the Tibetan bridge and Roc del Quer, and in winter for Grandvalira.

Things to do in Canillo

The Tibetan bridge

Opened in 2022, the bridge stretches 603 m across the Vall del Riu, up to 158 m above the ground at around 1,875 m, and it’s just 1 m wide. You can only get there on the official bus, from Canillo village or the Roc del Quer car park, and the bus is included with your ticket. Buy online or at the Canillo tourist office. Things to do in Andorra.

Mirador Roc del Quer

A 20 m walkway cantilevered out over the Canillo valley, with glass panels underfoot. It’s about 10 minutes’ walk from its car park on the Coll d’Ordino road, entry was €6 in 2026, and there’s a joint ticket with the bridge. Come early: it’s one of the best places in Andorra to watch the sun rise. Sunrise and sunset spots.

Mon(t) Màgic

Grandvalira’s summer family park at El Forn, reached by the Canillo gondola, has the 555 m Màgic Gliss toboggan, tubing, nature trails, and kayaks and pedal boats on Lake Forn. Its zipline runs 550 m down to the lake, 40 m above the ground at up to 80 km/h. Ziplines in Andorra.

Meritxell

Our Lady of Meritxell has been Andorra’s patron saint since 1873, and her sanctuary stands on the hillside above the main road. After the old church burned in 1972, Ricardo Bofill built the new one, opened in 1976, in Romanesque arches, slate and pale stone. It’s open daily from 9am to 8pm and free, and 8 September, her feast day, is the national holiday.

Sant Joan de Caselles

Just outside the village, this 12th century church is one of the Romanesque churches that best keeps its original character, with a Lombard bell tower and a timber roof. Andorra’s churches.

The Incles and Ransol valleys

From the end of the Vall d’Incles road, the walk to the Estanys de Juclar, Andorra’s largest lake, is 9 to 10 km return with about 500 m of climbing, and Estany de Cabana Sorda is about 5.5 km. To keep the valleys quiet, the Incles road closes to cars going up from mid June to mid September, 9am to 6pm, and the Ransol road in July and August, both with shuttles. Hiking in Andorra.

Four of Andorra’s six campsites are in Canillo parish: Camping Casal is open all year, and Santa Creu, Janramon and Font de Ferrosins in summer. At Christmas the village usually sets out a monumental nativity scene, with around 200 life size figures. The Palau de Gel, Canillo’s ice rink and pool, is closed for renovation until late summer 2027. See campsites and Christmas in Andorra.

Skiing from Canillo

The Canillo gondola lifts you from the village to El Forn, with easy slopes and the Mon(t) Màgic children’s circuit, and from there Grandvalira runs on to El Tarter and Soldeu.

Soldeu and El Tarter, both in the parish, sit at the foot of the slopes and make two of Andorra’s best ski bases. Soldeu has a gondola straight up to Espiolets and the country’s five star hotels, and hosts women’s World Cup giant slalom and slalom on 6 and 7 March 2027. El Tarter is quieter, with nursery slopes close to the village. See Grandvalira, Soldeu, El Tarter and ski hire.

Where to stay

Canillo village is the good value choice, with the gondola on the doorstep and the capital about 20 minutes away. For five star hotels on the slopes, look at Soldeu, home to the Sport Hotel Hermitage & Spa, Lodge Park Hotel & Spa and Serras Andorra, or try El Tarter for something quieter. Campers have more choice here than anywhere else in Andorra. See where to stay.

Getting there

From Andorra la Vella it’s about 20 minutes by car on the CG-2, through Encamp. National bus lines L3 and L4 stop in Canillo every 30 to 40 minutes from Monday to Saturday, and every 60 to 70 minutes on Sundays, and L3 runs on to Soldeu. From Ordino, the CS-240 crosses the Coll d’Ordino and comes down past Roc del Quer. See getting to Andorra.

Questions people ask

Where is Canillo in Andorra?

In the east of Andorra, about 20 minutes' drive from Andorra la Vella on the CG-2 towards France, past Encamp. The parish runs up the valley to El Tarter and Soldeu.

How do you get to the Tibetan bridge in Canillo?

Only on the official bus, from Canillo village or from the Roc del Quer car park, and the bus is included with your ticket. Tickets are sold online and at the Canillo tourist office.

Is Canillo good for skiing?

Yes. The Canillo gondola takes you from the village into Grandvalira's 215 km of pistes, and Soldeu and El Tarter, both in the parish, sit at the foot of the slopes.

Is the Palau de Gel in Canillo open?

Not at the moment. The ice rink and pool closed on 23 May 2026 for a major renovation, due to finish in late summer 2027. The padel courts stay open.

Choosing your base

Andorra is small, but where you stay shapes the trip: on the slopes, in the capital beside the spa and shops, or in a quiet mountain village.

Soldeu and El Tarter

The heart of Grandvalira and the natural choice for a luxury ski retreat. Ski-in, ski-out hotels, dining listed in the Michelin Guide and the best of the après are all on your doorstep. See the skiing guide.

Grau Roig

A small, peaceful base at around 2,100 m, surrounded by pine forest. It suits skiers who want to be first on the slopes and away from the busier villages.

Andorra la Vella and Escaldes-Engordany

Stay here to be close to Caldea, Avinguda Meritxell's shops, the old quarter and the widest choice of restaurants. The national bus station connects you to every resort. See the spa guide.

Ordino and La Massana

Quieter, more traditional parishes close to Pal Arinsal and Ordino Arcalís. In summer, they're the gateway to the Sorteny valley, the Tristaina lakes and horse riding at l'Aldosa. See summer in Andorra.

Pas de la Casa

On the French border at the top of Grandvalira, with the liveliest nightlife. It's best for groups who want après and late nights.

Arinsal

Friendly and good value, at the foot of Pal Arinsal, and popular with families and new skiers.

Questions people ask

Where is the best place to stay in Andorra for skiing?

Soldeu and El Tarter put you in the heart of Grandvalira with ski-in, ski-out hotels. Grau Roig is quieter and higher, Pas de la Casa is liveliest, and Arinsal suits families.

What are the best hotels in Andorra?

Hotels with five stars include the Sport Hotel Hermitage & Spa, Lodge Park Hotel & Spa and Serras Andorra in Soldeu, the Grau Roig Andorra Boutique Hotel & Spa, and the Andorra Park Hotel in Andorra la Vella.

Should I stay in Andorra la Vella or a ski resort?

Stay in a resort if skiing is the priority. Stay in Andorra la Vella or Escaldes-Engordany for Caldea, shopping, restaurants and easy bus links to every resort.

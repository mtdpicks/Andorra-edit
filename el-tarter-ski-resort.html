El Tarter ski resort

El Tarter is Soldeu’s quieter neighbour, a few minutes down the valley and linked to it on the slopes of Grandvalira. Families come for the gentle runs near the village, and plenty of others come for some of Andorra’s best après.

El Tarter at a glance

El Tarter sits on the valley floor between Canillo and Soldeu, at about 1,700 m. A gondola rises from the village into Grandvalira, and the slopes link straight across to Soldeu.

It's smaller and calmer than its neighbours, with stone buildings along the main road, a good choice of hotels and self catered apartments, and a relaxed feel that suits families and couples.

Skiing at El Tarter

Beginners and families

Nursery slopes sit close to the village, with wide blue runs above. Grandvalira's ski schools teach in English, and El Tarter has a snow garden for ages 3 to 6, the Bababoom circus learning area for under 12s, and a kindergarten for children aged 1 to 3.

Snowpark El Tarter

One of Grandvalira's three snowparks, right above the village. Freestylers can also ski across to the Sunset park at El Peretol.

Into the domain

From the top of the gondola you can ski to Soldeu, then on through Grau Roig to Pas de la Casa, or the other way to Canillo and Encamp. The official piste map is on grandvalira.com.

Ski passes

El Tarter is covered by the Grandvalira pass. Buy online for the best price, or choose the Andorra Pass season ticket for all three resorts. Passes and prices.

Après-ski in El Tarter

L'Abarset

At the foot of the slopes, L'Abarset was the standout après venue of 2025–26, hosting international DJs including Adam Beyer, Nina Kraviz and Paco Osuna at its outdoor parties. The fun starts around 4 pm.

Champagne on the piste

Higher up, on the Pi de Migdia slope in the El Tarter sector, the seasonal Veuve Clicquot In The Snow terrace serves champagne with oysters and live music. More après.

Ski hire in El Tarter

Many El Tarter hotels arrange hire for guests, so ask when you book. Viladomat's Soldeu shop is a few minutes up the valley, and you can book it online. Our ski hire guide.

On the valley road

El Tarter is about 30 minutes by bus from Andorra la Vella, on the main road towards Soldeu. Fly to Barcelona or Toulouse and take a direct coach to the capital. Getting to Andorra.

Hotels and apartments

El Tarter has a good mix of hotels and self catered apartments. See hotels in El Tarter and where to stay in Andorra.

Thermal water

Take the bus down to Escaldes-Engordany for Caldea's lagoons. Caldea, honestly.

Questions people ask

Is El Tarter good for beginners?

Yes. El Tarter has nursery slopes close to the village, wide blue runs, and ski schools that teach in English, with a snow garden for children aged 3 to 6.

Is El Tarter linked to Soldeu?

Yes. El Tarter and Soldeu are linked on the slopes as part of Grandvalira, around 215 km of pistes that also reach Grau Roig, Pas de la Casa, Canillo and Encamp.

Is El Tarter good for families?

Very. It's quieter than Soldeu or Pas de la Casa, and Grandvalira runs a snow garden for ages 3 to 6, the Bababoom circus learning area for under 12s, and a kindergarten for ages 1 to 3 in El Tarter.

How do you get to El Tarter?

El Tarter is on the main valley road between Canillo and Soldeu, about 30 minutes by bus from Andorra la Vella. Most visitors fly to Barcelona or Toulouse and take a direct coach to Andorra.
